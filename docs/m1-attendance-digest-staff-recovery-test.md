### Agreed notification behavior and remaining gaps

After a successful, complete check, an ordinary attendance reminder is needed only when a confirmed item needs attention. A complete check with no attention items suppresses the reminder. A failed or incomplete check must instead say **could not check** and retain the affected unresolved status; it cannot become an all-clear or invent a missing person. A failed-check warning is distinct from a confirmed missing-sign-in item. Receiving a message, provider acceptance, and even confirmed delivery do not resolve any attendance record, hourly clock question, warning or unfinished review day. Only the existing authorized correction/review paths can do that. **The absence of an email proves nothing** about attendance completeness or whether checking and delivery worked.

Existing protections are narrower than a finished notification service. `buildAttendanceDigest` and the digest/outbox tests cover complete-clean suppression, separate unavailable-read warnings and retained attention items. Capture and the separate synthetic email transport keep their original identities, content and failure history; email transport tests cover explicit safe retries, lost replies, concurrent attempts, storage failure, mismatched evidence and permanent provider-acceptance readback. A confirmed acceptance is not represented as inbox delivery. These are existing code/test protections, not evidence of a delivered message.

Automatic email retry and escalation are not implemented. The single-email transport makes one awaited provider request per authorized invocation; an explicit retry can reuse the original payload/key only inside its safe window. The current follow-up adds minimal Admin send and recovery controls behind the existing single-message approval gates, including an explicit retry of the same original ID and body. It does not add automatic retry. The tablet does not yet display an email-check or email-delivery failure warning. Its existing sign-in queue/connection notice and manager-review badge do not establish email health. Closing these notification gaps requires separate implementation and verification; this one-email test does not silently substitute staff troubleshooting for them.

## Staff Clock recovery

An employee with an unfinished earlier shift can choose **Start a new shift instead**, then propose the previous finish or select **I'm not sure**. The new clock-in gets its own permanent ID and actual start time. The old raw punch stays unchanged and unresolved. The proposal is not paid time and is not automatically approved. The new shift can be clocked out normally while the earlier one awaits a manager.

The browser keeps the complete original request in a separate recovery journal before sending. Reload and reconnect check authoritative evidence for that same request. An incomplete or mismatched receipt retains the original operation and blocks a replacement. Safe retries reuse its ID and timestamp. Neither the instructor queue nor existing Staff Clock storage is cleared.

Authorized managers review proposals inside the existing Staff Clock Admin section. Approval reuses the audited missed-punch correction path under its existing lock. The append-only recovery journal retains the employee's proposal, timestamps, original punches and manager decision. Approval validates the previous shift boundary and prevents impossible dates or overlaps; genuine overnight shifts retain their ordinary clock-out path. An unknown finish remains a question for the manager, never guessed hours. Staff Clock items remain separate from instructor attendance even when both appear in the digest.

Approval and rejection use an on-page confirmation showing the employee, both shift starts, employee-proposed finish, finish being approved, reason and reviewer. **Go back** preserves the unsent entries and writes nothing. **Confirm approval** or **Confirm rejection** first checks the current authenticated session and rereads the exact proposal. A changed proposal requires fresh review. Only after that confirmed, unchanged read does the app allocate and retain the decision IDs, then use the existing audited save and original-request recovery path. This interface applies to the gated real-use code as well as TEST; it does not enable live access.
